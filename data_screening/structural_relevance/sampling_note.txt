SAMPLING METHODOLOGY NOTE
AdJUST Project — Structural Relevance Screening

For the structural relevance screening stage, documents were randomly sampled
from every resource/document type present in the initial corpus. Sampling was
conducted using a fixed random seed (set.seed(123)) to ensure the sample is
reproducible.

Following sampling, the selected documents were manually reviewed by the two
Principal Investigators (PIs) to assess structural relevance -- i.e., whether
each document type was likely to contain articulations of policy paradigms.

This note is intended as a record of process for internal reference and future
replication.

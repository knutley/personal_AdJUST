## Notes

1. **Stemmed variants included:** Listed terms capture their common variants (e.g., "emission" includes "emissions", "emitting"; "forest" includes "forests", "forested").

2. **Compound phrases:** Multi-word terms (e.g., "climate change", "carbon capture and storage") should be matched as phrases where possible.

3. **Tier 3 usage:** These terms should ideally co-occur with Tier 1 or Tier 2 terms to confirm environmental policy relevance.
